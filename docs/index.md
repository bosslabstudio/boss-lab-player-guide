# Boss Lab Player Guide

**Boss Lab: Robot Battle Builder** is a free 2026 Android game where you build a robot boss from 60+ parts, give it an AI brain, and watch it auto-battle. Fight through a 100-boss campaign across 20 districts, or publish your boss to asynchronous Arena PvP where other players' bosses attack it while you're offline.

This site is a short, multilingual player reference. Each language page covers a different topic and links to the full guide on the official site.

> Written by **Boss Lab Studio, the developer of Boss Lab**. Game design, code and balancing are by the team; art and audio are AI-assisted.
